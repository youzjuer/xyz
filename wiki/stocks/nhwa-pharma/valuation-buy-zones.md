# Nhwa Pharma Valuation and Buy Zones

## Summary

基于 2026-05-13 附近数据，恩华药业不是高弹性成长股，而是**中枢神经用药细分龙头 + 稳定现金流 + 政策风险折价**的标的。

核心买点判断：

- **21-22 元附近：估值不贵，但不是舒服买点。**
- **18-19 元：合理买入区间。**
- **16-17 元：较好买点。**
- **15 元以下：如果基本面没有恶化，是高安全边际区。**

## Claim

恩华药业当前 TTM PE 约 20.5x-20.7x，forward PE 约 16x，估值不贵；但考虑到 2025 利润下滑、集采/医保控费、安全生产和核心产品增速放缓风险，买入需要更高安全边际。合理策略不是追，而是等到 18-19 元以下分批。

## Evidence

As of 2026-05-13 search snapshot:

- Stock price: about **RMB 21.42**.
- TTM PE: about **20.5x-20.7x**.
- Lixinger PE percentile: about **37.24%**.
- Lixinger PE reference percentiles: 20% / 50% / 80% around **19.56x / 21.97x / 24.85x**.
- 2026E net profit consensus reference: about **RMB 1.33 billion**, EPS about **RMB 1.31**, implying forward PE around **16x** near RMB 21.37.
- 2025 revenue: RMB **5.911 billion**, YoY **+3.75%**.
- 2025 net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB **1.057 billion**, YoY **-7.54%**.
- 2026 Q1 revenue: RMB **1.610 billion**, YoY **+6.58%**.
- 2026 Q1 net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB **317 million**, YoY **+5.56%**.

## Buy zones: 002262.SZ

| Zone | Price | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Avoid chasing | > RMB 23 | 不追，除非利润增速明显恢复到双位数且风险消除 |
| Current fair/watch | RMB 20-22 | 可观察或很小仓，赔率一般 |
| Fair buy zone | RMB 18-19 | 合理分批买入区间 |
| Attractive buy zone | RMB 16-17 | 较好买点，可提高计划仓位 |
| High safety-margin zone | < RMB 15 | 若非基本面恶化导致，是高安全边际区 |

Core answer: **18-19 元以下开始有吸引力，16-17 元较好，15 元以下舒服。**

## Valuation logic

Use EPS / forward PE as rough anchors.

If 2026E EPS is about RMB **1.31**:

| Scenario | Target PE | Implied price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Chasing / expensive for this profile | 18x+ | RMB 23.6+ |
| Fair | 15x | RMB 19.7 |
| Attractive | 13x | RMB 17.0 |
| High margin of safety | 11-12x | RMB 14.4-15.7 |

The company does not deserve the same valuation as high-growth innovative pharma unless profit growth accelerates. A more conservative 13x-15x forward PE is more appropriate while policy and product-price risks remain.

## Conditions that move the buy zone up

The acceptable buy price can move higher if several of these occur:

1. 2026 H1/Q2 net profit growth accelerates to **10%+**.
2. Operating cash flow recovers after Q1 decline.
3. 集采/医保控费 pressure on core products is milder than feared.
4. Safety-production整改 is completed with no material penalty or production disruption.
5. 神经类 and new products keep high growth and contribute meaningful incremental profit.

If these are confirmed, then:

- RMB **20-21** can become an acceptable fair buy zone.
- RMB **18-19** becomes a strong buy zone rather than just fair.

## Conditions that move the buy zone down

The buy zone should move lower if several of these occur:

1. 2026 Q2 profit growth weakens or turns negative.
2. Core anesthesia/psychiatric products face meaningful new price cuts.
3. Safety-production issues lead to penalty, production suspension, or longer整改.
4. Operating cash flow remains weak.
5. Stock rerates above 18x forward PE while growth remains single digit.

If these occur, then:

- Fair buy zone should move down to **RMB 16-17**.
- Comfortable buy zone should move down to **RMB 14-15**.

## Position sizing rule

- New buyer: wait for RMB 18-19 or lower before meaningful position.
- Defensive/pharma allocation: can start a very small tracking position around RMB 20-21 if willing to accept lower expected return.
- Existing holder: can hold below RMB 22 if thesis is stable cash flow, but trim if valuation rises above RMB 23-24 without profit acceleration.
- Heavy position: avoid overconcentration due to policy and compliance risks.
