# BYD Valuation and Buy Zones

## Summary

基于 2026-05-14 对原分析的修正：**比亚迪 A 股 80 元以下确实有吸引力，但不应作为基准买点去死等。**

更现实的 A 股买点框架：

- **88-92 元：现实首买区**，如果投资者想要比亚迪龙头敞口，可以开始分批。
- **82-86 元：强买区**，通常需要额外负面冲击。
- **80 元以下：压力/恐慌机会区**，吸引力强，但需要负面情景叠加，不能作为唯一买入锚点。

原来的 75-80 元买点过于静态估值化，低估了比亚迪的龙头溢价、出海期权和市场对已知利空的提前定价。

## Market-expectations correction

The key question is not only what price gives enough margin of safety. It is whether that price is realistic under the current market narrative.

For BYD, a fall from about RMB 98 to RMB 80 requires roughly a 19% drawdown. That is possible, but not the base case unless several negatives stack together: weak Q2 profit, monthly sales failing to recover, price-war deterioration, sector de-rating, or export/tariff shock.

BYD is priced through three layers:

1. **Traditional automaker earnings**: currently under pressure from price war, sales slowdown, and margin compression.
2. **China EV leader premium**: still supports valuation while scale, cost advantage, and share leadership are not broken.
3. **Export/globalization optionality**: April 2026 export volume of 135k units keeps the global EV platform narrative alive.

Therefore, RMB 80 is an attractive stress-case price, not the most practical base-case first buy point.

## Claim

在 Q1 2026 净利润同比大幅下滑、1-4 月销量同比仍下滑、A/H 股 PE 分位仍偏高的情况下，比亚迪需要更高安全边际。除非业绩恢复得到确认，否则不应在 A 股约 98 元、H 股约 98 港币附近追买。

## Valuation logic

The corrected buy-zone logic combines static valuation with market-expectations probability:

| Scenario | Target valuation logic | A-share rough price implication |
|---|---|---:|
| Light watch | about 20x forward PE | RMB 82-88 |
| Fair buy | about 18x forward PE | RMB 75-80 |
| Comfortable buy | about 16x forward PE | RMB 68-72 |
| Excellent opportunity | about 14-15x forward PE | RMB 60-66 |

This is not a precise DCF. It is a margin-of-safety framework for a cyclical-growth leader whose near-term profit trend is weak.

## Conditions that move the buy zone up

The acceptable buy price can move higher if several of these occur:

1. Monthly sales stabilize above **380k-400k** units.
2. Q2 or Q3 net profit returns to roughly **RMB 8-10 billion** per quarter.
3. Gross margin stops falling.
4. Exports continue to grow without major tariff/regulatory pressure.
5. Domestic price war eases.

If these are confirmed, then:

- A 股 **85-90 元** can become acceptable.
- H 股 **90-95 港币** can become acceptable.

## Conditions that move the buy zone down

The buy zone should move lower if several of these occur:

1. Q2 net profit continues to fall sharply YoY.
2. Monthly sales stay below **330k** units.
3. Domestic price war intensifies.
4. Overseas exports hit tariff, localization, or regulatory barriers.
5. Market starts valuing BYD as a traditional cyclical automaker.

If these occur, then:

- A 股 fair buy zone may move down to **RMB 65-70**.
- H 股 fair buy zone may move down to **HKD 68-75**.

## Position sizing rule

- New buyer: do not start with full position; use price bands and earnings confirmation.
- Existing low/medium position: can hold and wait for data.
- Existing heavy position above **15%-20%** portfolio weight: reduce risk unless the user explicitly wants aggressive EV exposure.
