# BYD Stock Analysis

## Summary

比亚迪仍是中国新能源车里最强的公司之一，但截至 2026-05-13 的判断是：**好公司，弱周期，估值还没给足安全边际。**

当前不适合因为“龙头”身份而无脑追买。更合理的策略是等价格回落，或者等销量和利润率恢复得到确认。

## Identity

- Company: 比亚迪股份有限公司 / BYD Company Limited
- A-share: `002594.SZ`
- H-share: `1211.HK`
- Sector: 新能源汽车、电池、储能、汽车产业链垂直整合

## Latest evidence snapshot

Captured: 2026-05-13.

### 2026 Q1 financials

Official HKEX Q1 report showed:

- Operating revenue: RMB **150.225 billion**, YoY **-11.82%**.
- Net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB **4.085 billion**, YoY **-55.38%**.
- Net profit after extraordinary items: RMB **4.148 billion**, YoY **-49.24%**.

Interpretation: profit fell much faster than revenue, so the market should focus on margin pressure, price competition, product mix, and cost/expense absorption rather than only headline sales.

### Sales and exports

2026 April production/sales reports showed:

- April NEV sales: **321,123 units**.
- Jan-Apr 2026 NEV sales: **1,021,586 units**, YoY **-26.02%**.
- April NEV exports: **135,098 units**.
- April battery and energy-storage installed capacity: about **20.977 GWh**.

Interpretation:

- Export volume is strong and remains an important second growth curve.
- Domestic/overall sales have not yet confirmed a YoY trend reversal.
- April had sequential repair, but this is not enough to declare a full recovery.

### Valuation snapshot

As of 2026-05-13 search results:

- A-share `002594.SZ`: price about **RMB 98.60**, TTM PE about **32.6x**, PE percentile about **87.7%**.
- H-share `1211.HK`: price about **HKD 98.15**, TTM PE about **28.8x**, PE percentile about **86.8%**.
- H-share forward PE reference: about **19x**.

Interpretation: valuation still prices BYD as a high-quality leader. That is reasonable long term, but less attractive while Q1 profit is down sharply.

## Thesis

### Bull case

- BYD has one of the strongest vertical-integration models in global EVs.
- Battery, powertrain, manufacturing scale, and model-cycle breadth support cost leadership.
- Export growth can offset some domestic price-war pressure.
- Multi-brand matrix can cover mass market, premium, off-road, and ultra-premium segments.
- If domestic price war eases and overseas sales keep scaling, earnings can recover meaningfully.

### Bear case

- Q1 2026 showed profit deterioration much larger than revenue deterioration.
- Jan-Apr sales were still down materially YoY.
- Domestic EV competition remains intense.
- Export growth faces tariff, localization, regulation, and geopolitical risk.
- Current A/H valuations are not low enough if profit recovery is delayed.

## Current view

### Long-term quality

Strong. BYD remains a core EV leader and deserves tracking in any China EV watchlist.

### Current business trend

Weak-to-recovering, not fully reversed. The key issue is whether monthly sales and profit margin can stabilize after Q1's profit drawdown.

### Current valuation

Not cheap. A-share is less attractive than H-share; H-share has better relative value but still needs either price pullback or earnings confirmation.

### Operating conclusion

- New position: do not chase near the 2026-05-13 price area.
- Existing low/medium position: can hold and observe.
- Heavy position: consider reducing risk if position weight exceeds about 15%-20% of total portfolio.
- Short-term 1-3 month trade: not the best elasticity target unless monthly sales and sector sentiment improve.

## Trigger checklist

### Upgrade triggers

- Monthly sales stabilize above **350k** units.
- Stronger confirmation if two consecutive months approach or exceed **380k-400k** units.
- Q2 or Q3 quarterly net profit returns to roughly **RMB 8-10 billion** level.
- Gross margin stops falling.
- Export growth remains strong without major tariff/regulatory impact.
- Price war eases.

### Downgrade triggers

- Q2 net profit continues to fall sharply YoY.
- Monthly sales remain below **330k** units.
- Domestic price war expands.
- Overseas export growth hits tariff or regulatory barriers.
- Market begins to value BYD more like a cyclical automaker than a high-growth EV leader.
